# Prism — Model Selection

## Selected Models

### 1. Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct
- **HuggingFace:** `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct`
- **Size:** 7B parameters
- **Architecture:** Standard dense decoder-only Transformer (Qwen2.5 architecture)
- **Training:** SFT on math data with instruction tuning, supports both Chain-of-Thought (CoT) and Tool-Integrated Reasoning (TIR)
- **Context Window:** 128K tokens
- **Role in Prism:** Primary Qwen math model. Instruction-tuned for direct prompting, strong CoT reasoning, good fine-tuning target for LoRA PRM experiments.

---

### 2. DeepSeekMath-7B-Instruct
- **HuggingFace:** `deepseek-ai/deepseek-math-7b-instruct`
- **Size:** 7B parameters
- **Architecture:** Standard dense decoder-only Transformer (DeepSeek architecture)
- **Training:** SFT with instruction tuning, CoT prompting with `\boxed{}` final answer format
- **Context Window:** 4K tokens
- **Role in Prism:** Same-scale counterpart to Qwen2.5-Math-7B-Instruct. Enables cross-family comparison at identical parameter count. Standard HuggingFace chat template integrates directly into existing backend.

---

### 3. Phi-4-mini-reasoning
- **HuggingFace:** `microsoft/Phi-4-mini-reasoning`
- **Size:** 3.8B parameters
- **Architecture:** Dense Transformer with **Grouped-Query Attention (GQA)** and a 200K token vocabulary (significantly larger vocab than models 1 & 2)
- **Training:** Trained *exclusively* on synthetic math data generated by DeepSeek-R1, using reinforcement learning with reasoning budgets
- **Role in Prism:** Architectural contrast — GQA produces different attention patterns than standard MHA. Different training philosophy (synthetic-data-only RL) vs. standard SFT. Strong AIME 2024 and MATH-500 scores at 3.8B.

---

### 4. InternLM2-Math-Plus-7B
- **HuggingFace:** `internlm/internlm2-math-plus-7b`
- **Size:** 7B parameters
- **Architecture:** InternLM2 architecture — different rotary positional encoding scheme and tokenizer from Qwen/DeepSeek families
- **Training:** 2-stage SFT, functions as both a **solver and a reasoning step verifier/scorer**
- **Role in Prism:** Most directly relevant to the PRM angle — can score intermediate reasoning steps in addition to generating them. Architectural contrast via different positional encoding. Matched size to other 7B models enables fair cross-architecture comparison.

## Narrative for Prism

These 4 models are chosen to enable meaningful cross-model explainability comparisons:

- **Same-size cross-family comparison:** Models 1 vs. 2 (both 7B, Qwen vs. DeepSeek families) — do different training corpora and tokenizers produce different internal reasoning patterns at the same scale?
- **Training methodology:** Models 2 vs. 3 (SFT vs. synthetic RL-only) — do different training regimes produce different internal reasoning trajectories visible via logit lens?
- **Architecture + function:** Model 4 (InternLM2 verifier) — does a model trained to verify reasoning steps show distinct gradient attribution patterns compared to pure solvers?

Prism's explainability suite (token confidence, attention heatmaps, logit lens, gradient attribution, hidden state norms) applied across all 4 models on identical math problems forms the core experimental contribution.

## Backend Integration Notes

- All 4 models are standard HuggingFace `AutoModelForCausalLM` — compatible with existing `model_manager.py` and `remote_model_client.py`
- All fit on a single Colab free-tier T4 GPU (15GB VRAM) when loaded in `float16` or `bfloat16`
- Explainability endpoints (attention, logit lens, hidden states) assume a standard transformer architecture — all 4 qualify
- Add each model to `MODEL_CONFIGS` in `backend/model_manager.py` with appropriate prompt formatting per model's chat template
